🧠 AI DETECTION FEATURES:
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The AI detector analyzes:
• URL length (long URLs are suspicious)
• IP addresses (instead of domain names)
• Suspicious TLDs (.tk, .ml, .ga, .cf, .gq, .zip)
• Phishing patterns (@ symbol, multiple hyphens)
• Suspicious keywords (login, verify, urgent, banking, etc.)
• HTTPS security
• Multiple dots/slashes
• Overall risk scoring

Result includes:
• Status: Safe / Malicious / Invalid
• Confidence: 0-100%
• Risk Score: 0-100
• Detailed reasoning
